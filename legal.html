Privacy policy

What we store

Your cart, your language choice and any demo orders or showroom bookings are saved in your browser’s localStorage. They never reach a server, and you can delete them at any time by clearing this site’s data.

Cookies and tracking

None. There are no cookies, no analytics, no advertising pixels and no social widgets — which is why you didn’t see a cookie banner.

Third parties

Fonts, Three.js and every other library are served from this website, so your IP address is not shared with Google, CDNs or anyone else. The only external links are the “Directions” buttons, which open Google Maps when you click them.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct and delete your data. Because all data stays on your device, you exercise these rights yourself in your browser settings.

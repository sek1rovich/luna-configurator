Questions

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to make a sofa?

Two to six weeks depending on the fabric: linen 2–3 weeks, bouclé and velvet 3–4, leather 5–6. You get the exact date once your order is confirmed.

Is delivery really free?

Yes, to every EU country plus Norway, Switzerland and the UK: we deliver, carry it up, assemble it and take the packaging away. Rural addresses included.

Can I return a made-to-order sofa?

Custom furniture is exempt from the EU’s 14-day right of withdrawal, but we offer a 30-day return anyway. We collect it ourselves; you only pay 10% of the price to cover the tailoring.

What does the 10-year warranty cover?

The frame, the springs and all mechanisms. Fabric and filling are covered by the statutory 2-year EU guarantee. If anything happens, we repair it at your home.

How do instalments work?

Ten equal monthly payments at 0% APR through our payment partner. The first payment is taken when you order.
